## Research

**Undergraduate Researcher — BCML, Kwangwoon University** · Mar. 2026 – Present

- **First-author accepted abstract — iWAIT 2027**  
  *Towards Re-Encoding-Aware Evaluation of Subject Leakage in Pose-Based Action Recognition.*  
  Studied privacy evaluation of pose-based action recognition, examining how re-encoding a pose representation can affect measurements of subject information without changing the information it contains.
- **Current research questions (ongoing):** When can action evaluation improve VLA decisions? In particular, how do **failure observability** and **avoidability** affect the usefulness of verification or alternative action selection?
- **Exploring:** VLA policies, model-based reinforcement learning, world models, and robot learning. These are research interests and ongoing investigations, not claims of completed robotics experiments.
